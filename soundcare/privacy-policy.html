Privacy Policy — Soundcare: Speaker Cleaner & Volume Booster

Effective date: 17 November 2026

Developer: Shahid

Privacy contact: developer.aaqeel@gmail.com

Soundcare provides speaker tone sessions and local audio playback with sound controls. This policy describes the app’s current Android implementation. Advertising is not currently enabled in the iOS implementation.

Data on your device

The app stores preferences, saved tone presets, session history, and listening observations on your device. Audio files you choose are processed locally for playback; the app does not upload these audio files to the developer. The app does not record microphone audio or provide microphone measurements. It does not require an account.

You can manage saved information in the app. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes app-owned local data, subject to your device’s backup and restore settings. Original audio files you selected are managed separately by you and your device.

Advertising

The Android app uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show banner, native, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads. Google may collect and share IP addresses, device and advertising identifiers, interactions with advertisements, and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, security, and fraud prevention. IP addresses may be used to estimate general location. These advertising practices are separate from locally processed audio files.

See Google’s Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
and Google’s advertising information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Where required, the app requests advertising consent through Google’s consent platform. You can revisit available consent choices through “Advertising privacy choices” in Settings when that option is required and available. Your device also provides advertising privacy controls. Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily remove ads.

Completing an optional rewarded advertisement grants a temporary ad-free period. There is no paid ad-removal purchase.

Sharing and retention

The app does not operate a developer server to receive your audio files, presets, or session history. Google and its advertising partners handle advertising information under their own policies and retention practices. If you contact the developer, information you provide is used to respond and address your request.

Your choices

You can delete locally stored information, manage advertising choices, and contact the developer about privacy questions or requests. Rights available to you depend on your location and the information involved. Requests concerning Google-held advertising information may also be directed to Google through its privacy tools.

Children

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information through the app or a privacy inquiry, please contact Shahid at developer.aaqeel@gmail.com. Advertising partners’ collection and controls are described in the advertising section above.

Updates and contact

Changes to data practices will be reflected in an updated policy with a revised effective date. For privacy questions or requests, contact Shahid at developer.aaqeel@gmail.com.
